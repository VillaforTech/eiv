# Community conduct

EIV welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints.
Contributions should make the work more understandable, reproducible or correct.

- Critique claims, code and evidence with specific examples.
- Ask questions in good faith and explain technical terms when useful.
- Respect contributors' time, attribution, privacy and accessibility needs.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, disclose private information or publish
  another person's confidential material.
- Do not fabricate results, independent review or consensus.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful material, pause a discussion or limit
participation. Decisions should be proportionate, explained to affected people
where possible, and open to relevant new information. Good-faith technical
criticism and negative results are welcome.

Report concerns through the maintainer's contact options on
[their profile](https://github.com/VillaforTech); avoid posting private details
in a public issue. A private contact route will be listed when available.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces operated for the project. See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for how changes
are reviewed.
